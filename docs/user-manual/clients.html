Clients

Add and manage the people you support — care plans, details, and their full communication timeline.

The Clients page lists the people you support: ID, name, caregiver, counsellor, and their next scheduled call. Search by ID, client, caregiver or counsellor, and filter by whether a next call is scheduled.

Adding a client

  1. Click Add client.
  2. Enter the client's name and phone, and the caregiver's name and phone. The caregiver's phone is the number dialled when you start a call, and the number WhatsApp messages go to — enter it in international format (e.g. +447700900000).
  3. Pick the agent that should handle this client's chat. Without an agent the chatbot cannot respond.
  4. Save with Create.

The client page

Open any client to see everything about them in one place:

  • Detailed information — contact details, the assigned navigator (CTN), and the caregiver, with their relationship to the client (daughter, neighbour, paid carer…) and how involved they usually are.
  • The agent — which agent answers this client's chats, and a switch to turn answering off. While it is off, messages still arrive and are recorded; what stops is the agent replying. You give a reason, and the platform keeps who turned it off and when.
  • How they want to be contacted — contact preferences chosen from a standard set, so they mean the same thing across the team, plus any you add yourself.
  • Next call — when the next appointment is, with shortcuts to reschedule.
  • Care plan — upload a PDF care plan, view the current one, or send it to the caregiver via WhatsApp (if enabled for your platform). PDFs up to 5 MB.
  • Background — free-text notes in Markdown: who they live with, how they get about, what to avoid.
  • Handover note — what the next person should know to make their first call feel like continuity rather than a fresh start.
  • Protocols — the programme this client works through, chosen under Edit. Only these appear in their call panel; a new client starts with none. See Protocols.
  • Executed protocols — the question sets completed in past calls, with their answers.
  • Communication timeline — every alert, call, meeting and chat with this client, grouped by month and week. Collapse sections or filter by type; Now jumps to the present.

Conversation history

From the timeline (or the client page) open a specific day to read the full conversation between the client/caregiver and the agent — including voice messages, which you can play right there. See Conversations for reviewing and giving feedback.

Privacy
Care plans, recordings and voice notes are private. Only admins and the client's own navigator can open them — links alone are never enough.
