The calendar

See scheduled meetings at a glance and book new ones in month, week or day view.

The Calendar shows your scheduled meetings at a glance and lets you book new ones. Switch between Month, Week and Day views, jump to Today, and step forwards or backwards.

Booking from the calendar

  1. Click any empty slot.
  2. Pick the client and the meeting type (and protocol, if applicable).
  3. Schedule. Conflicts are checked — you can't book two meetings in the same time range.

Days with meetings show indicators. Clicking a meeting opens it in the detail panel beside the calendar, so you can read it, work it or reschedule it (pending meetings only) without losing the month you were looking at. Cancelled calls stay on the calendar, struck through and greyed.

Why the panel closes when you change view
Switching between Month, Week and Day — or stepping forwards and backwards — closes the open panel on purpose. Changing the view is navigation, not selection: carrying the panel across meant that switching to Month reopened an event you had not clicked, often one no longer on screen.
Tip
The navigator assistant can book meetings for you — try asking it to “schedule a follow-up with … next Tuesday at 3pm”.
