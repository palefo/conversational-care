Conversational Care — User Manual

How care teams use the platform: from the first sign-in to alerts, calls and configuration.

Conversational Care is a platform that helps care teams support people with chronic conditions and their family caregivers. AI agents handle routine conversations over WhatsApp, SMS or web chat, while the platform keeps your team in the loop — scheduling structured phone calls, tracking every conversation, and raising alerts whenever something needs a human.

Who this manual is for

This guide is for the people who use the web application day to day:

RoleWho they areWhat they see
NavigatorA care team navigator (CTN) or counsellor supporting a caseload of clients.Their own clients, calls, calendar, conversations and alerts.
AdminThe platform administrator for your organisation.Everything navigators see, for all clients — plus Settings, Users and Agents.
Test userAn account linked to one client, used to try out that client's chatbot.Only the audio chat for their linked client.

Clients — the people receiving care, together with their caregivers — are not users of this application. They interact with the platform through WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls, or a chat link, and your team sees those interactions here.
